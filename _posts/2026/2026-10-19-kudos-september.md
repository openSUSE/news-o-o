Welcome to our monthly report from the [openSUSE Kudos recognition platform](https://kudos.opensuse.org), where we take a moment to put a spotlight on the people who stepped up, helped others, and made this community a little brighter.

During the month of **September 2026**, **14** users received **14** kudos, and **152** badges were awarded to **136** contributors. You can see [stats](https://kudos.opensuse.org/stats) for this and previous months.

## Spotlight

September spread its thanks evenly, with fourteen people each receiving a kudo: [@adrianSuSE](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/adrianSuSE), [Bernhard Wiedemann (@bmwiedemann)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/bmwiedemann), [Dominique Leuenberger (@dimstar)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/dimstar), [Geetansh Goyal (@geetxnshgoyal)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/geetxnshgoyal), [@hennevogel](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/hennevogel), [Imobach Gonzalez Sosa (@IGonzalezSosa)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/IGonzalezSosa) and eight more.
