Welcome to our monthly report from the [openSUSE Kudos recognition platform](https://kudos.opensuse.org), where we take a moment to put a spotlight on the people who stepped up, helped others, and made this community a little brighter.

During the month of **October 2026**, **23** users received **58** kudos, and **209** badges were awarded to **173** contributors. You can see [stats](https://kudos.opensuse.org/stats) for this and previous months.

## Spotlight

If October had a scoreboard, [Indonesian Team (@indonesian-team)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/indonesian-team) would be holding it, with **18 kudos**, more than three times what anyone else collected. Most of them (**14**) landed in Event & Release Organizing. That alone accounts for **18** of the month's **58** kudos. Second place goes to [Lubos Kocman (@lkocman)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/lkocman) with **6**. Third place was shared by [Asia summit (@asia-summit)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/asia-summit), [Douglas DeMaio (@ddemaio)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/ddemaio), [Kukuh Syafaat (@cho2)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/cho2) and [Siska Iskandar (@siskaiskandar)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/siskaiskandar), on **3 kudos** each.

A milestone is worth stopping for: [@jubalh](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/jubalh), [@mcepl](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/mcepl), [@mnhauke](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/mnhauke), [@rjschwei](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/rjschwei), [@tinita](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/tinita) and [@tiwai](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/tiwai) reached **[100 Tumbleweed Contributions](https://kudos.opensuse.org/badge/tumbleweed-100)** and [@krop](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/krop) and [@pluskalm](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/pluskalm) reached **[1000 Tumbleweed Contributions](https://kudos.opensuse.org/badge/tumbleweed-1000)**. Let me remind everybody we literally started to count in July, so we're talking 1000 contributions since July 2026. Wow. <strong>Congratulations!</strong> Your paces pushed us to create a <a href="https://github.com/openSUSE/kudos-badges/pull/36">10k tier badge with a beautiful emerald color</a>.

Not all of it is code, either. Documentation, translation, artwork, moderation and event work keep openSUSE usable and welcoming for everyone, and this month [@aryulianto](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/aryulianto), [@belphegor_belbel](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/belphegor_belbel), [@darianrizaludin](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/darianrizaludin), [@diamond_gr](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/diamond_gr) and [@cho2](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/cho2) earned **[Wiki Contributor](https://kudos.opensuse.org/badge/wiki-1)** and [@ddemaio](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/ddemaio), [@cho2](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/cho2), [@jimed-rand](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/jimed-rand) and [@lkocman](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/lkocman) earned **[Wiki Bronze](https://kudos.opensuse.org/badge/wiki-10)**.
