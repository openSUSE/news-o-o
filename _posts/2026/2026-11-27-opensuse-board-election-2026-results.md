Voting closed last night at midnight UTC, and this morning I had the pleasure of sending out the results of the **openSUSE Board Election 2026**. Martin Pluskal, Jeff Mahoney and Ihno Krumreich have been elected to the openSUSE Board. Congratulations, Martin, Jeff and Ihno!

## The openSUSE Board Election 2026 in numbers

Out of 548 eligible openSUSE Project members, 268 cast their votes. That is a turnout of 48.9%, so almost every second member found a few minutes to vote.

Five candidates ran for three seats, and this is how the votes ended up:

| Candidate | Votes |
|---|---|
| **Martin Pluskal** (elected) | **207** |
| **Jeff Mahoney** (elected) | **197** |
| **Ihno Krumreich** (elected) | **164** |
| Shobha Tyagi | 98 |
| Geetansh Goyal | 62 |
{: style="margin-bottom: 1.5em"}

There is a clear margin between the elected candidates and the rest of the field, so Martin, Jeff and Ihno go into their term with strong support from the membership. Congratulations to Ihno in particular for defending his seat. He joined the Board last year as the candidate with the most votes after those elected, when Jeff was appointed chair in place of Gerald Pfeifer and his seat opened up. This time Ihno earned it directly at the ballot box. And Jeff has shown that he belongs on the Board even without the chair title.

I would like to thank all five candidates for stepping forward. It really takes courage to put your name out there, answer questions on the mailing list and let the community decide. Shobha and Geetansh, thank you for running. I hope to keep seeing you around the project as much as ever, and Geetansh, I already noticed your kind congratulations on the list just minutes after the announcement went out.

## Before somebody asks about the chair

I decided to answer this one before anyone had to ask. Jeff is stepping down from his role as Board chair and [leaving SUSE](https://lists.opensuse.org/archives/list/project@lists.opensuse.org/thread/KTSZ43XFOBM6CMEHV5H3LGI4OWAMMC2K/), and we are currently awaiting SUSE's appointment of a new chair.

At its [last meeting](https://lists.opensuse.org/archives/list/project@lists.opensuse.org/thread/YWGTJO6E3AU2P4AJSA76NKTKGI7T6S7J/), the Board agreed that a brief transition period for Jeff would be completely acceptable if the election turned out in his favor. And it did. The topic was discussed during that meeting, it just didn't make it into the minutes.

## What comes next

The newly elected Board members are invited to the next Board meeting for a handover with the current Board. It takes place on Monday, December 7, from 13:30 to 14:30 UTC, and the details are in the [openSUSE calendar](https://calendar.opensuse.org/teams/board/events/meeting).

If you like digging through the past, the full election history, including today's results, lives on the [openSUSE Board history](https://en.opensuse.org/openSUSE:Board_history) wiki page.

Those of you who read the [Board meeting minutes](https://lists.opensuse.org/archives/list/project@lists.opensuse.org/thread/YWGTJO6E3AU2P4AJSA76NKTKGI7T6S7J/) already know that there is no rest for us election officials. We will be running yet another vote, this time on the governance proposal, from December 7 to December 21, right after Hack Week 26.

## Interested in helping with the next election on December 7?

The Election Committee is looking for new members to join our [team of election officials](https://en.opensuse.org/openSUSE:Election_officials). The work comes in waves, with a busy period around the yearly Board election and the occasional ad hoc vote like this one, and quieter time in between to improve the election rules and process. We value quality and reliability over quantity, so we are looking for people who want to stay around long term.

If you are an openSUSE member and that sounds like you, email [election-officials@lists.opensuse.org](mailto:election-officials@lists.opensuse.org) and tell us what you would bring to the team and what you would like to see changed in how we run elections. And yes, there is a [fancy kudos badge](https://kudos.opensuse.org/badge/election-official) in it for you ;-)
