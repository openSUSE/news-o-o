The [openSUSE Leap 16.1](https://news.opensuse.org/2026/11/03/leap-unites-traditional-and-immutable/) release retrospective [survey](https://survey.opensuse.org/) will be extended for one more week, until Nov. 30.

If you installed, upgraded to, migrated to or just tried Leap 16.1, please take a few minutes to fill out the survey at [survey.opensuse.org](https://survey.opensuse.org/). 

Every answer counts, whether your experience was good, bad or indifferent.

Your feedback helps shape the next release and Leap 16.1 already contains changes that came straight out of the Leap 16.0 retrospective.

What we especially want to hear about: 

  - **Immutable Mode:** Leap Micro is now part of Leap itself. Does choosing Immutable Mode in Agama, updating with transactional-update and rolling back work the way you expect?

  - **Migrating from Leap Micro 6.2:** The path through the openSUSE Migration tool is still experimental. Success stories and failures are both valuable.

  - **The appliance images:** Tell us how the self-install, VM, cloud and encrypted raw images from get.opensuse.org worked for you.

  - **Agama:** The installer has matured a lot since 16.0. Tell us where it still gets in your way.

  - **Upgrading from 16.0:** Did the upgrade go smoothly? What broke?

  - **Desktops and software:** KDE Plasma 6.6, GNOME 48, LXQt 2.4, Xfce on Wayland, Python 3.14, Podman 5.8 and the rest of the updated stack.

  - **Hardware and baseline:** The x86-64-v2 requirement, SELinux as the default and 32-bit support being off by default.

  - **The release process itself:** How the release was announced, documented and communicated, including the new version diff tool.How to take part

Go to [survey.opensuse.org](https://survey.opensuse.org/) and help us in our development for release retrospective; help make Leap 16.2 better for everyone!
