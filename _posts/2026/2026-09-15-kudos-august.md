Welcome to our monthly report from the [openSUSE Kudos recognition platform](https://kudos.opensuse.org), where we take a moment to put a spotlight on the people who stepped up, helped others, and made this community a little brighter.

During the month of **August 2026**, **17** users received **24** kudos, and **419** badges were awarded to **251** contributors. You can see [stats](https://kudos.opensuse.org/stats) for this and previous months.

## Spotlight

Top of the list in August: [Bernhard Wiedemann (@bmwiedemann)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/bmwiedemann) with **5 kudos**, more than twice what anyone else collected. Most of them (**4**) landed in Code & Engineering. That alone accounts for **5** of the month's **24** kudos. Second place was a three-way tie at **2 kudos** each: [@bigironman](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/bigironman), [Gertjan Lettink (@knurpht)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/knurpht) and [Martin Schreiner (@mschreiner)](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/mschreiner). Behind them, thirteen more people each picked up a kudo of their own, and all of them are listed below.

A milestone is worth stopping for: [@dirkmueller](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/dirkmueller), [@jengelh](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/jengelh), [@krop](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/krop) and [@pluskalm](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/pluskalm) reached **[100 Tumbleweed Contributions](https://kudos.opensuse.org/badge/tumbleweed-100)**. That is the kind of steady, long-haul contribution that rarely makes headlines. Congratulations!

Not all of it is code, either. Documentation, translation, artwork, moderation and event work keep openSUSE usable and welcoming for everyone, and this month [@aplanas](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/aplanas), [@bmwiedemann](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/bmwiedemann), [@jimed-rand](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/jimed-rand) and [@lkocman](https://kudos.opensuse.org/user/lkocman) earned **[Wiki Contributor](https://kudos.opensuse.org/badge/wiki-1)**.
